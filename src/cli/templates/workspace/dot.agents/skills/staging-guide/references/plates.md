# Plates

- **A `location` reference is the master, and the root of each camera axis is a window cut out of it** — the place from its main camera position, wide enough to hold every setup on its axis. The root is `adapters.imageCrop` of it (`konte adapter show imageCrop`). A setup that moves the camera off the axis is a root of its own: generate its plate from the master, framing declared new, or give its axis a master.
- **Build each frame from the plate of the setup it is `within`** (`plate-unnested`) — an edit model takes the set's scale off image 1, and no prose asking for a tighter view moves it; nesting is what makes a `close` come back closer than its `medium`. Two forms nest. A **cut**: the parent plate passed as is to a model that reads a previous frame (`cut` in `konte adapter show`), written as a cut in from that frame — the camera moves, so eye height, perspective and the size of what is near change as they do on a set. A **window**: an `imageCrop` inside the parent's — a zoom from where the parent's camera stands, flat and enlarged. Build a `close` as a cut; keep windows for a small step. A root (`within: null`) is cut from the master or generated.
- **A plate fixes how much of the set is in frame, and is passed at canvas size** — a plate larger than the canvas reads as a picture inside the frame, its edges drawn as a window. Cut every window with the setup's `holds` inside it: a crop with none of them is another room. Where the shot needs a frame the plate does not hold, build another frame on the axis; off the master's axis, carry its setting and light onto a new one and say the framing is new (the adapter's guide has the marker). A setup whose shots keep doing that is two setups.
- **A setup two or more generated shots share owes a plate** (`setup-unrealized`) — declared under `plates` in animatic.tsx and keyed by the roster id (`animatic:plate.<setupId>`). Feed `plates.<id>.image` as the keyframe's first image input; a setup one shot uses needs none, unless the piece cuts between it and the other exposed size of its `within` axis (`axis-unrealized`). An asset declared here and **not** returned is an intermediate — never review work, and its name may not be a setup id.
- **Every shot stands on the place** — the plate where its setup has one, `reference:<location>` where it does not (`setup-unconsumed`, satisfied by any one of its keyframes); the plate is built from that same reference (`plate-unanchored`). A place held by prose alone is a different place every take. Exempt: a shot with no generative step, and a plate handed over whole as a file.
- **Draw a plate empty** — a pose or gesture baked into it is inherited by every shot on that setup, and the shot's own performance has to fight it.
- **A plate is `{ image, prompt }`, and its `prompt` is one sentence of what that frame holds** — contents only, per window; hand every framing decision to the picture. With no sentence the place is lost whenever the model drops the picture; a sentence that frames beats the picture's own framing. It calls every landmark in the setup's `holds` by its `promptDepiction` (`plate-unnamed`). Write `plates.<id>.prompt` into the keyframe's own prompt, in the adapter's notation (`plate-undescribed`) — the sentence names the contents, the surrounding line names the picture and gives it the framing.
