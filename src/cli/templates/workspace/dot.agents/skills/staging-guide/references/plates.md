# Plates

- **Generate every plate from a reference image; never use `imageCrop` to make one.** A root setup (`within: null`) uses the `location` master, with the setup's framing declared new. Carry the master's setting and light onto each camera axis.
- **Generate each child plate from the plate of the setup it is `within`** (`plate-unnested`) — pass the parent plate as is to a model that reads a previous frame (`cut` in `konte adapter show`), and write a cut in from that frame. Match the child's framing, camera height and perspective.
- **A plate fixes how much of the set is in frame, and is passed at canvas size** — a plate larger than the canvas reads as a picture inside the frame, its edges drawn as a window. Include the setup's `holds`. Where the shot needs a frame the plate does not hold, generate another setup's plate; never crop the plate inside the keyframe (`plate-recropped`).
- **A setup two or more generated shots share owes a plate** (`setup-unrealized`) — declared under `plates` in animatic.tsx and keyed by the roster id (`animatic:plate.<setupId>`). Feed `plates.<id>.image` as the keyframe's first image input; a setup one shot uses needs none, unless the piece cuts between it and the other exposed size of its `within` axis (`axis-unrealized`). An asset declared here and **not** returned is an intermediate — never review work, and its name may not be a setup id.
- **Every shot stands on the place** — the plate where its setup has one, `reference:<location>` where it does not (`setup-unconsumed`, satisfied by any one of its keyframes); the plate is built from that same reference (`plate-unanchored`). A place held by prose alone is a different place every take. Exempt: a shot with no generative step, and a plate handed over whole as a file.
- **Draw a plate empty** — a pose or gesture baked into it is inherited by every shot on that setup, and the shot's own performance has to fight it.
- **A plate is `{ image, prompt }`, and its `prompt` is one sentence of what that frame holds** — contents only; hand the camera's place and range to the picture — where the figures stand is the panel's. With no sentence the place is lost whenever the model drops the picture; a sentence that frames beats the picture's own framing. It calls every landmark in the setup's `holds` by its `promptDepiction` (`plate-unnamed`). Write `plates.<id>.prompt` into the keyframe's own prompt, in the adapter's notation (`plate-undescribed`) — the sentence names the contents, the surrounding line names the picture and gives it the framing.
