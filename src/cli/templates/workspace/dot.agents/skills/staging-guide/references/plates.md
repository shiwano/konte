# Plates

- **Generate every plate from its `location` master (`plate-unanchored`); never use `imageCrop` to make one.** A root setup (`within: null`) declares its framing new. Carry the master's setting and light onto each camera axis.
- **Generate a child plate from its `within` setup's plate** (`plate-unnested`) — pass the parent plate as is, beside the master, to a model that reads a previous frame (`cut` in `konte adapter show`), and write a cut in from that frame. Match its framing, camera height and perspective. Alone, the parent is enlarged to a blur.
- **A plate fixes how much of the set is in frame, and is passed at canvas size** — a plate larger than the canvas reads as a picture inside the frame, its edges drawn as a window. Include the setup's `holds`. Where the shot needs a frame the plate does not hold, generate another setup's plate; never crop the plate inside the keyframe (`plate-recropped`).
- **A setup two or more generated shots share owes a plate** (`setup-unrealized`) — declared under `plates` in animatic.tsx and keyed by the roster id (`animatic:plate.<setupId>`). Feed `plates.<id>.image` as the keyframe's first image input; a setup one shot uses needs none, unless the piece cuts between it and the other exposed size of its `within` axis (`axis-unrealized`). An asset declared here and **not** returned is an intermediate — never review work, and its name may not be a setup id.
- **Every shot stands on the place** — the plate where its setup has one, `reference:<location>` where it does not (`setup-unconsumed`, satisfied by any one of its keyframes). A place held by prose alone is a different place every take. Exempt: a shot with no generative step, and a plate handed over whole as a file.
- **Empty a plate of figures** — a pose baked into it is inherited by every shot on that setup, whose performance has to fight it. Keep the set the framing reaches — a bare wall or gradient fixes neither place nor look.
- **A plate is `{ image, prompt }`, and its `prompt` is one sentence of what that frame holds** — contents only; hand the camera's place and range to the picture. With no sentence the place is lost whenever the model drops the picture; a sentence that frames beats the picture's own framing. It calls every landmark in the setup's `holds` by its `promptDepiction` (`plate-unnamed`). Write `plates.<id>.prompt` into the keyframe's own prompt, in the adapter's notation (`plate-undescribed`), inside a line that names the picture and gives it the framing.
