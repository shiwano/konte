# A piece that shows a process

## One subject, start to finish

- **One subject changes from its before to its finished state** — both on screen; a pitch names the subject and the finished thing.
- **Lens: `satisfying-process` when the method is the pleasure, `transformation` when the reveal is** — a `transformation` shows no full view of the finished thing before its reveal.
- **Steps in the order they really happen** — compress by montage, never by reordering.
- **Each step changes the subject visibly** — a step whose result is not in its last frame is cut or merged.

## Pace the steps

- **The obvious version is every step at even pace** — linger on the hard step, sprint through the routine ones.
- **The `rhythm` role repeats the method faster** — the same action in shorter shots.
- **The final image is the finished subject, held.**
