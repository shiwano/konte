# A mood piece

## The skeleton is the lens's roles

- **Lens: `mood-piece`** — atmosphere → motif → variation → peak → fade.
- **Test: delete a role's shots** — if no motif is dropped and no step of the build goes missing, the role has no idea yet.

## A motif carries it

- **One or two motifs — an object, a gesture, a light, a color** — "the neon sign stutters on", never "urban loneliness"; planted first, returned to in every later role.

## Build by repetition and intensification

- **A return never comes back unchanged** — it raises scale, speed, light, the number of figures, or the camera's distance; the same frame with one thing changed reads as a return, an unchanged copy as a loop.
- **The peak is the motif at its most** — the most distinct frame and the fastest cutting. Spend nothing bigger before it.
- **A quiet stretch before the peak** — a held frame; the peak is measured against it.

## Pitching

- **Pitch a map, not a chain of events** — one line each: the world, the motif, how the peak lands.
- **Never slip an incident in** — a pitch with an event nobody asked for is the first one cut.
