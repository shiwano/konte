# Critique with no subagent

The pass you run yourself when the human sends you on without a subagent — narrower than `konte-direction-critic`'s. Done = every finding below fixed, or kept on purpose in `brief.tolerances` with its reason; an _(advisory)_ one taken or said why not.

## Out of reach

- **Don't judge whether a shot reads** — an action unambiguous, a payoff landing, a sequence rising: its evidence is a first viewer's impression, and you already know what every shot means.
- **Don't hand that gap to the human** — "please check whether shot 03 reads" is your missing pass wearing a question mark.

## What still holds — artifact against artifact

An axis survives when **both sides are on the page**, and the check is whether they match:

- **Hook** _(advisory)_ — shot 01's `action` puts `brief.hook` on screen.
- **Deletion** — cut the shot: does anything downstream stop working?
- **Merge** _(advisory)_ — are trigger and reaction separate shots that one action would carry?
- **Setup / payoff** — every plant collected, every collection planted.
- **Causality** — position, possession, movement and state connect across a continuous-space stretch; the world's ledger never resets.
- **Lineup** — each shot's `lineup` holds everyone its `action` acts, and a shot that moves someone declares the `lineupTo` it ends on.
- **Recurrence** — a noun several shots' `action` names has a `characters` / `props` / `locations` entry.
- **Set** — a thing of the set an `action` leans on (a desk, a window) is one of its location's `landmarks`.
- **Implied presence** — a figure the `action` cannot happen without (the rower of a moving boat, whoever serves at a counter) is named and rostered.
- **Brief compliance** — an element `brief.outOfScope` bans is a finding wherever it shows; whatever `brief.tolerances` accepts is closed on every axis.
- **Risk concentration** — does the payoff rest on one demanding shot with nothing to fall back on? What a given model can hold is its guide's question (`production-guide`).

## How to look

- **Re-open `direction.ts` and read the shots in order, as written** — never from memory; memory is where your intent lives.
- **Report a shot only when it is formally valid and still weak as a cut** — `framing`, `duration`, lens `role`, roster ids and the pacing lines are the direction check's.
- **What the human already accepted is settled** — `konte inspect direction`. It is the fixed reference the rest must meet, so where settled and unsettled work clash the finding lands on the unsettled side.
