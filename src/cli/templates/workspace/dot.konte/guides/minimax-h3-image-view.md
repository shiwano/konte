# MiniMax H3 Image — views

A location's view image, painted over its coarse — `back` from the master alone.

## Coarse

- konte declares `reference:view.<location>.<view>#coarse` for every view but `back` a location with a master declares, until `views` returns that view made without it: the master lifted to 3D and drawn from the view's camera, what the master never saw left flat grey. It is free and generated by `konte generate reference` before any view is written; `konte path reference:view.<location>.<view>#coarse` is the image.

## Inputs

```ts
views: ({ reference, coarse }) => {
  const roomLeft = asset("roomLeft", imageMinimaxH3ImageView, {
    image1: coarse.room.sideLeft,
    image2: reference.room,
    prompt: {
      view: {
        room: "sitting room",
        camera:
          "The camera stands against the window wall at standing eye height, looking straight across the room toward the door wall.",
        layout:
          "The fireplace stands on the left wall, seen at a steep angle, with the red wing chair before it at the near left edge; the panelled door is at the far end of the room, beyond the green wing chair.",
        look: "its wallpaper, woodwork, palette, line and dim indigo light",
        coarseFill: "the room's floor, dark patterned wallpaper, bookshelves and furniture",
      },
      details: "The furniture stands unoccupied, the room still.",
      style: "…",
    },
  });
  const roomBack = asset("roomBack", imageMinimaxH3ImageView, {
    image1: reference.room,
    prompt: {
      view: {
        room: "sitting room",
        camera:
          "The camera stands with its back to the fireplace wall at standing eye height, looking straight across the room toward the opposite wall.",
        layout:
          "The tall window is on the left wall, with the green wing chair's back before it at the near left edge; the far wall faces the camera, panelled below and papered above, a bookcase against it.",
        look: "its wallpaper, woodwork, furniture, palette, line and dim indigo light",
      },
      details: "The furniture stands unoccupied, the room still.",
      style: "…",
    },
  });
  return { room: { sideLeft: roomLeft, back: roomBack } };
},
```

- `image1` is the coarse and `image2` the master, with `coarseFill`; `back` takes the master as `image1`, without `coarseFill`.
- `room` is the place's noun, no article.
- `camera` is where the camera stands, at what height, and what it faces — by the wall at its back, never a thing out of frame: named, it comes back in the foreground.
- `layout` is where each landmark lies, in place words — the wall it stands on, near or far, at a frame edge, out of frame. No proportions. Over a coarse it is read off the coarse; for `back` it also names what fills the walls the master never showed, else they come back bare.
- `look` is what the master carries over: its materials, palette, line and light.
- `coarseFill` is what the coarse's grey becomes: the room's surfaces and the furniture the master shows.
